Who we are

Read the full brief →

“Uwezo” is Swahili for capability. It has been the standard we are held to since a small team started guarding premises in Ntinda in 2011.

Today we field vetted officers, an armoured cash-in-transit fleet, a manned control room, and vault custody for cash, documents and precious metals. Every deployment is written up, every movement is logged, and every custody is issued against a verifiable receipt. We are licensed by the Uganda Police Force and carry full liability cover.

One chain of custody

Whether the asset is a payroll, a deed or a kilo of gold, it moves through the same four recorded stages — and the holder can check its status at any point.

STAGE 01

Receive

Counted and sealed in the client's presence. A reference and receipt are issued on the spot.

STAGE 02

Custody

Held in an allocated position under dual control, with periodic recorded checks.

STAGE 03

Verify

Anyone holding the reference can confirm the record is genuine and current, online.

STAGE 04

Release

Handed back only against identity and signature. The custody is closed and logged.
