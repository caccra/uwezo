About the firm

Uwezo is Swahili for capability. It has been the standard we're held to since a small team started guarding premises in Ntinda, fifteen years ago.

The founding

A small team began guarding premises in Ntinda in 2011. Fifteen years on, that word — capability — still defines how we show up for every client.

What started as a handful of officers has grown into a firm of over 200 trained, vetted security professionals, serving clients from gated homes and schools to banks, NGOs and large commercial sites across Uganda.

We are licensed by the Uganda Police Force under the Private Security Organisations framework, and carry full liability cover. Every deployment is written up, every movement is logged.

Mission, vision, values

Mission

To protect the lives, property and livelihoods of every client we serve — with professionalism, honesty and genuine care for the people behind every assignment.

Vision

To be the security company Ugandans recommend to each other — trusted not just for what we do, but for how we do it.

Values

Integrity, reliability and respect for everyone we work with — client, officer or partner. People's safety depends on the standard we hold.
