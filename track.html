What the statuses mean

  • Scheduled
    Collection booked and a crew assigned. Reference and PIN issued to you.
  • Collected
    Assets sealed, counted and loaded. Chain of custody opened.
  • In Transit
    Under armed escort with live GPS tracking to the control room.
  • At Facility
    Received at the vault or bank, undergoing verification and count.
  • Delivered
    Handed to the named recipient against signature. Custody closed.
  • Exception
    A delay or discrepancy has been logged — the control room will contact you directly.
